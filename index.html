Memory-Greedy Fictitious Play in Cyclic Three-Action Games

The note derives the exact step sequence and duality gap for a memory tie-breaking rule in rock–paper–scissors, extends the analysis to weighted cyclic three-action zero-sum games, and gives a four-action example where pure best-response choices cannot make progress.

02

Manuscript · 2026

A sharp higher-order Cheeger inequality

For finite undirected weighted graphs, this paper obtains exactly k disjoint sets with conductance bounded by a constant times √(λk log(k + 1)). The logarithmic dependence is optimal up to an absolute constant.
